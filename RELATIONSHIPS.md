# Optional relationship artifact

components/relationships.json is a separately versioned optional research artifact, not a change to Guide Pack v2. The consumer copy is startup-tarot/data/relationships.json. Keep bytes and review-register hashes synchronized.

Public nodes and typed edges have stable IDs. Each edge requires sources, a factual statement, basis and limits. Keep event, publication and checked dates distinct; unknown dates remain unknown. Public records exclude people and private knows edges. Portfolio membership does not prove shared rounds, acquaintance or current fit. Shared-round analysis requires identified round participation.

Current coverage is incomplete: 19 nodes and 13 edges. Independent source review and expansion remain editorial responsibilities. Consumer tests do not establish real-world completeness. Private founder notes and fictional topology are separate consumer data and never public research evidence.
