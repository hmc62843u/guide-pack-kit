# Review: assess three kinds of quality

**Inputs:** changed facts, connections, suggestions, actual sources and any evaluation results.
**Outputs:** review-register.json, SOURCES.md, applicable notices and a separate assessment of research, matching and suggestions. Return unresolved content to its owning stage; omit unresolved additions from the distributable pack.

- Read the existing review bases before changing selected records. Review new/changed facts and components for factual support, source terms, protected expression and distribution scope. If unresolved, omit the proposed addition from the distributable package and report the gap; do not silently approve it or invent publisher permission. A factual/reuse assessment is not independent legal clearance.

- Maintain review-register.json with path, name, reviewed status, actual review date, meaningful reuse_basis and reviewed_sha256 of the exact saved bytes for each selected record/component. Use an available native hash tool such as Windows Get-FileHash. Preserve hashes only for unchanged bytes. If hashing or review is unavailable, report the release review as incomplete rather than claiming success. Keep SOURCES.md hashes and bases synchronized and update source-specific notices when needed.

| Review | Check | Evidence to report |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Research | Claim/source agreement, dates, omissions, unknowns and limitations. | Reviewed records/claims, disagreements and unresolved sources. |
| Matching | Supported topics and conditions; independently established positive, negative and unknown expectations. | Case counts and expected/actual differences; gaps in coverage. |
| Suggestions | Understandability, practical effort, relevance, cautions and observable learning. | Actual reviewer/founder feedback and its limits; no fabricated user study. |

- Check research support independently from rule behavior. Establish expected clear matches, nonmatches, insufficient-information and boundary cases before comparing output. A compatible file is not proof of good research or useful suggestions. Record actual reviewed cases, sample sizes, disagreements and results; claim no population accuracy or startup outcome benefit without evidence.

Handoff honest quality assessments to packaging. Updating a declaration is reporting a review, not performing one. Keep rights review distinct from factual accuracy and user usefulness. A review performed by the same author/assistant is not an independent review.
